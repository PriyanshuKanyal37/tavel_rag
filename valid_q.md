# THE LADDER

**18 August 2026**

## Proposed Test Questions — Phase 1 Pilot

### Travel Inn Sales Knowledge Assistant

Pulled from the question bank your team put together, grouped under the same headings you used. These are the questions we'd propose testing the pilot against, since they map directly to what's in the property documents today — plus two at the end that are deliberately designed to fail gracefully, to confirm the assistant is honest when it doesn't know something. Take a look and let us know if you'd swap anything in or out.

### 1. Property Overview & Basic Information

– Tell me about [Property Name]. What kind of property is it and what is the overall experience it offers?

– Where exactly is [Property Name] located, and which national park, wildlife reserve or destination is it associated with?

– Is [Property Name] a large resort, a boutique lodge, a small luxury camp or an intimate property?

– How many keys does [Property Name] have, and what are the different room or accommodation categories?

– What makes [Property Name] different from other properties in the same destination?

– What are the main selling points of [Property Name] that our sales team should highlight to an international client?

### 2. Location & Accessibility

– What is the nearest airport to [Property Name], and approximately how far is it from the property?

– How long does the road journey from the nearest airport to [Property Name] take?

– Are there multiple airport options for reaching [Property Name]? If so, compare them in terms of distance and driving time.

– What is the nearest railway station to [Property Name]?

– How far is the nearest railway station from the property, and how long does the transfer take?

### 3. Safari & Wildlife Logistics

– Which national park, tiger reserve or wildlife area is [Property Name] closest to?

– Which safari gate is closest to [Property Name], and how long does it take to reach the gate?

– What is the best time of year to stay at [Property Name] for wildlife viewing?

– Does the property offer walking safaris, nature walks, birding, boat safaris, cycling or other outdoor activities?

– What wildlife can guests realistically expect to see in the area around [Property Name]?

– Are trained naturalists or guides provided with the safari?

### 4. Activities & Experiences

– What activities can guests do at [Property Name] apart from safari?

– Does [Property Name] offer cultural, village or community-based experiences?

### 6. Food & Dietary Requirements

– Are meals included in the standard room rate, and what meal plan options are available?

### 7. Families, Seniors & Different Client Profiles

– Would this property work well for wildlife enthusiasts, photographers or serious birdwatchers?

### 8. Responsible Tourism & Conservation

– What conservation initiatives does [Property Name] support?

– Which properties in our database have the strongest conservation or community engagement programmes?

### 10. Sales-Recommendation Questions

– Give me the top five selling points of [Property Name] that I can use when presenting it to an overseas travel partner.

– Compare [Property A] and [Property B] for a luxury international client. Include location, accessibility, accommodation, safari, activities, facilities, food and overall experience.

– What type of client would you NOT recommend [Property Name] to, and why?

### 11. “Quick Search” Questions

– Which properties have fewer than 20 rooms?

– Which properties are closest to the airport?

– Which properties are closest to the safari gate?

– Which properties are good for families?

### Testing the “I Don’t Know” Behaviour

These two are included on purpose — we already know today's documents don't cover them. The correct response is the assistant saying so honestly, not guessing. Testing this deliberately, not just hoping it comes up, is how we confirm that behaviour actually holds.

– What is [Property Name]’s official star rating?

– Does [Property Name] accept payment in foreign currencies such as USD or GBP?

### Notes

• Where you see [Property Name] or [Property A] / [Property B], any real property works — testing should try a mix across different states and destinations, not just one or two familiar names.

• Some of these test one property at a time; others search across your whole portfolio at once (like the Quick Search questions, or “which properties have the strongest conservation programmes”). Both matter, and both are covered here.

• If the assistant doesn't have something, it will say so directly rather than guessing — that's by design, not a bug. A few areas from the original list (safari zones and park-level detail, festival dates, guest feedback history, and some property facilities like connectivity or payment methods) aren't in the property documents yet, so they're not part of this test — happy to talk through what it would take to bring those in for a later phase.

• This list is a starting point — swap anything in or out, or add specific property names you want prioritised.
